Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Riayat ("the app") is provided by Riayat Codes, Lahore, Pakistan. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

  • Device identifiers and advertising data: collected automatically by Google AdMob (advertising ID, device information) to display ads.
  • Usage/analytics data: collected automatically by Google Firebase Analytics (app interactions, screen views, device and app information).
  • Local data you create: your wishlist, favourite brands, theme preference and cached product listings are stored only on your device. This data is not uploaded to us.

How we use information

To display advertisements, understand app usage, and improve the app. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or precise location. The app has no account system.

Third parties

We share data with Google (AdMob and Firebase Analytics) for advertising and analytics. These services may collect device identifiers and usage information under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings and opt out of personalised ads there. Analytics data is retained and handled by Google under its privacy policy.

Your choices

You may opt out of personalised advertising in your Android device settings under Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of personalised ads.
